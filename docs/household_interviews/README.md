# Household Interview Evidence

This folder preserves household interview source evidence.

Rules:

- Interview files are attributed source evidence, not automatically joint household policy.
- Preserve the speaker, interview date, status, uncertainty labels, and unresolved items.
- Do not silently promote ESTIMATE, TARGET, CONDITIONAL, UNKNOWN, or individual preference into verified fact or joint policy.
- Later reconciliation may compare interview evidence with institution-observed data, calculated history, another household member's interview, and explicit joint decisions.
- When an interview is completed or superseded, preserve history rather than rewriting what the person originally said.
- Raw statements, credentials, account numbers, security codes, and source documents do not belong here.
